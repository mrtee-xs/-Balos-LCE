STANCE: Strong yes

3. The offer is your main lever: "nailing the offer is the single strongest lever on your business success" (S114). Package Balos not as a chatbot, but as the "Dubai After-Hours Sales Engine": we install it, load your inventory, integrate your WhatsApp, and guarantee 10 qualified AED 2M+ viewings in 30 days or you get a full refund.

THE RISK I SEE: The founder folds on price during the call because of lack of conviction, defaulting back to free.
WHAT WOULD CHANGE MY MIND: Showing me that CAC on paid clients is 5x higher than pilots and that conversion on free pilots is over 90%.
WHAT I'D DO THIS WEEK: Use the CLOSER framework on today's call: diagnose their current lead leakage after 6 PM, state the price, shut up, and handle the hesitation with the 30-day guarantee.
QUESTION FOR THE FOUNDER: If you don't believe Balos can book 10 viewings in 30 days to justify $6k, why are you selling it?
