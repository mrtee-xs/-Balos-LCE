## Chair notes

### Who Moved and Why
- **Graham** moved from Conditional Yes (7/10) to Strong Yes (9/10) on Option B after seeing the mathematical impossibility of hitting $100k under Option A within 94 days and a 10-client cap.
- **Buffett**, **Martell**, and **Hormozi** hardened their stance against Option A (dropping it to 1–2/10), calling it a fatal trap that causes founder burnout and zero revenue.
- The entire council converged on a refined version of Option B: **$6,000 Upfront Setup ($3,000 downsell if resisted) + $2,000/mo Retainer**, backed by a qualified 30-day viewing guarantee.

### Cruxes Resolved
1. **Case Studies vs. Risk Reversal**: Resolved unanimously. The combination of a live mobile demo during the call + 30-day performance refund guarantee completely removes the need for existing case studies.
2. **Pricing Structure**: Pitch $6,000 upfront in full. If the prospect hesitates, immediately offer terms: $3,000 upfront today + $3,000 on Day 30 upon booking 5 viewings. Never offer free.
3. **Delivery Protection**: Solved by setting a standard 3-hour onboarding limit per client and using first-client cash to hire an onboarding VA.
