# Brief: Balos LCE Offer & Closing Strategy for $100k in 100 Days

## 1. The Decision
How should the founder structure and pitch the commercial offer on today's sales call with a Dubai brokerage to maximize immediate close rate and hit $100,000 revenue in the next 94 days without breaking delivery capacity?

### Options on the Table
- **Option A (Founder Lean): 14-Day Free/Reimbursable Pilot to $3,000/mo Retainer.** Offer a 14-day zero-risk trial converting automatically into a $3,000/month contract if Balos books at least 5 qualified AED 2M+ buyer viewings.
- **Option B (Paid High-Ticket Setup + Performance Retainer): $6,000 Upfront Implementation + $2,000/mo Retainer.** Charge a $6,000 onboarding and custom WhatsApp/CRM integration fee plus $2,000/month, backed by a 30-day "10 qualified viewings or 100% money-back" guarantee.
- **Option C (Pay-Per-Qualified-Viewing / Commission Share): $1,000 Escrow Deposit + $250 per Verified Viewing.** Brokerage places a $1,000 deposit; pays $250 per completed qualified buyer viewing, plus a 10% share of agency commission upon deal closing.

## 2. The Business
- **Product:** Balos Lead Conversion Engine (WhatsApp AI qualifying leads in <15s, Golden Visa AED 2M+ screening, tri-modal viewing booking, Supabase operations dashboard).
- **Target:** Boutique/mid-size Dubai real estate brokerages (10–30 agents spending heavily on Property Finder, Bayut, Meta ads).
- **Stage:** Live product, Day 6 of 100-day sprint. Revenue: $0 (assumed). Margins: ~90% software/API costs (assumed).

## 3. Resources & Constraints
- **Team:** Solo founder handling engineering, sales, and client onboarding.
- **Capacity:** Can personally manage 5–10 deep client implementations over 90 days.
- **Goal:** $100,000 in cash collected within 94 days (Day 100).

## 4. Founder's Lean
Leaning toward Option A to reduce friction on the initial calls and secure proof-of-concept case studies.
